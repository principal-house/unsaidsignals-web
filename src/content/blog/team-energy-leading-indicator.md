When team energy drops, quality and speed drop next. The problem is timing: by the time metrics show the impact, momentum is already expensive to recover.

Energy is observable in small signals: delayed decisions, defensive updates, and lower initiative. Teams rarely call this out directly, but patterns make it visible.

A weekly pulse paired with narrative analysis gives leaders time to intervene while trust is still intact and before burnout hardens into attrition.
