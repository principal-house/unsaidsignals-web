In many teams, the highest cost work is invisible: unresolved assumptions, avoided conflict, and unspoken uncertainty.

These gaps create rework loops. Teams move, then correct, then realign. Over time, this burns focus and confidence.

When leaders receive clear weekly narratives about friction and trust dynamics, they can fix systems instead of patching symptoms.
