# UnsaidSignals Landing Page Iteration Plan

## Current State Analysis

The landing page is functional but needs refinement to match Principal.house design standards.

### What's Working
- Clear value prop: "See what your team isn't telling you"
- Dark, professional aesthetic
- Three core signals clearly defined
- Simple 3-step process

### What Needs Improvement (Based on DESIGN.md)

1. **Too Generic**
   - Placeholder social proof
   - Generic CTAs ("Get Started")
   - No real human quotes/stories

2. **Missing Emotional Resonance**
   - Doesn't capture the "Oh shit" moment of discovery
   - Too feature-focused vs problem-focused
   - No concrete examples

3. **Lacks Specificity**
   - How long does setup take?
   - What does the first week look like?
   - What exact insights do I get?

4. **Trust Building**
   - No security/privacy messaging
   - No founder story or "why we built this"
   - Generic placeholder logos

## Priority Improvements

### Phase 1: Immediate Impact (Today)
1. Replace generic hero with specific problem statement
2. Add real quotes from team leaders
3. Show actual insight examples (redacted)
4. Strengthen CTAs with specific outcomes

### Phase 2: Trust & Credibility
1. Add privacy/anonymity section
2. Include founder perspective
3. Real testimonials or case studies
4. Pricing transparency

### Phase 3: Conversion Optimization
1. Interactive demo or sample report
2. FAQ addressing objections
3. Comparison to alternatives
4. ROI calculator

## Key Messages to Emphasize

**The Problem (in human words):**
- "I found out my best engineer was interviewing elsewhere... from LinkedIn"
- "My team says everything's fine in standups, then three people quit"
- "I'm managing through a screen and have no idea what's really happening"

**The Promise:**
- "Know what's brewing before it boils over"
- "2 minutes from your team, insights that change everything"
- "The truth your 1:1s will never surface"

## Design Principles for This Page

1. **10-second test**: Can a stressed VP understand value immediately?
2. **One breath pitch**: "Anonymous weekly pulse reveals what your team won't say directly"
3. **Respect their time**: No complex explanations, just "here's what you'll know"
4. **Stressed user friendly**: Big buttons, clear path, no thinking required

## Next Steps

1. Rewrite hero section with human problem
2. Create 3 example insights (visual)
3. Add security/privacy section
4. Replace placeholder social proof
5. Strengthen CTAs throughout

Remember: Every element should help a stressed team leader think "Yes, I need this NOW."
